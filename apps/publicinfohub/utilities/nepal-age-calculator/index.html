Nepal Age Calculator

What this tool doesUse an AD or BS birth date, and compare it with a chosen Gregorian reference date. The age result uses the Gregorian calendar after BS conversion.

Use the birth date printed on your official document. For eligibility, use the exact age-calculation rules in the recruiting authority’s notice; some use a cut-off date and may calculate age differently.
