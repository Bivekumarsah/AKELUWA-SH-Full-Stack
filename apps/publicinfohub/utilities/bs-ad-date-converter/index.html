BS ↔ AD Date Converter

What this tool doesValidate a Nepali date and display the equivalent international date, or convert AD to BS. Historical and future conversions depend on the calendar dataset.

Try 2024-04-13 AD → 2081-01-01 BS. Accepts English or Nepali digits; use YYYY-MM-DD. Calendar support is limited to the open-source library’s dataset, and future BS month lengths may change.
