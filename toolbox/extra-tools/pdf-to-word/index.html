PDF to Editable Word (DOCX)

A free, browser-based text-first PDF to Word converter. It extracts available text into DOCX; complex tables, fonts, images, columns and page layout are not preserved.

Important informationThis produces an editable text-oriented DOCX, not an exact visual reproduction. PDF scans may have no text layer; use the OCR extractor before copying results into Word.
