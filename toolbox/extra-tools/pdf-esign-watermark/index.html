PDF eSign & Watermark

Add a handwritten signature illustration to a document or apply a transparent text watermark. Not equivalent to a certificate-backed digital signature.

Important informationThe output contains an appearance-only mark. It does not authenticate a signer, and editing a cryptographically signed document may invalidate the original signature.
